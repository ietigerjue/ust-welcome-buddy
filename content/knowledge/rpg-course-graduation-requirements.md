This note summarizes common Research Postgraduate (RPG) course and graduation requirement concepts for HKUST MPhil and PhD students. Academic requirements are official policy and vary by school, department, program, intake year, and student status. Always verify with the HKUST program catalog, Academic Registry, FYTGS, your department, your supervisor, and your program office.

## Start from your official program requirements

RPG students should first check the official HKUST postgraduate program requirements for their own program and intake year. Do not copy requirements from another department or a previous year.

Look for:

- Required coursework.
- Professional development requirements.
- English language requirements.
- Seminar requirements.
- Qualifying examination or PQE requirements.
- Thesis research registration.
- Department-specific rules.

If anything is unclear, ask your department or program office early.

## Required courses

Required Courses are the normal credit-bearing courses students usually think of as "courses." The required number of credits and approved course list differ by program.

Some departments list exact courses; others say approved coursework and expect students to follow department advice. RPG students should attend department orientation, ask senior students, and confirm with the program coordinator before finalizing course choices.

## GTA training

Graduate Teaching Assistant training prepares RPG students to work as TAs. The source mentions PDEV6800 as a common GTA training-related course with training and instructional delivery components.

Typical things to check:

- Whether you need to enroll in a GTA training course.
- Whether you must complete training before serving as TA.
- Whether your department requires one or more instructional delivery assessments.
- Who signs or approves the TA-related forms.
- Whether oral English requirements affect TA eligibility.

TA assignment practices vary by department. For questions, contact the relevant TA coordinator, postgraduate program coordinator, course instructor, supervisor, or department office.

## PDC requirement

Professional Development Course (PDC) requirements may include university-level and school or department-level components.

The source mentions PDEV6770 variants for university-level PDC requirements and department-specific professional development courses or seminars. Students may need to register for the correct course in Student Center and separately enroll in eligible workshops or activities.

Practical reminders:

- Make sure the correct PDC course appears in Student Center.
- Use the official PDC system or department instructions to check requirement categories.
- Some workshops may count even if registration happens on another platform, but verify before relying on them.
- Sign in or complete attendance requirements properly.
- Keep a personal record of completed workshops and hours.

## English language requirement

RPG students may have English language requirements covering oral communication and academic writing or presentation skills.

The source discusses ELPA, LANG5000, and later language courses such as LANG5005 variants. Exact rules, exemptions, consequences, and course codes can change by intake and department.

If you are unsure whether you passed, were exempted, or need to take a language course, confirm with the official English language requirement page or your department.

## Postgraduate seminar

Many departments require RPG students to enroll in seminar courses and attend a required number of seminars. Course codes and attendance rules vary.

New RPG students should check:

- Which seminar course to enroll in each semester.
- Whether the seminar has a changing suffix or section.
- How attendance is recorded.
- Whether make-up options exist.
- How many semesters are required.

## PQE

PQE usually means PhD Qualifying Examination. The format can vary widely: written exam, oral exam, proposal defense, coursework performance, or a department-specific combination.

Practical reminders:

- Know whether your program requires PQE.
- Know the expected timeline.
- Ask what happens after a failed attempt or delayed attempt.
- Confirm whether MPhil students, PhD students, and transfer students have different rules.

This is a high-impact academic requirement, so use official department and HKUST policy as the source of truth.

## Thesis research

Thesis Research is the core of MPhil or PhD study. Students may need to enroll in a thesis research course every regular term, with course codes depending on department and degree level.

The source gives chemistry examples, but each student should use their own program's course code. Thesis research registration is important even when there is no normal classroom component.

## Concentration

Some programs offer concentrations. A concentration is a specialization track that may appear on the transcript if its requirements are met. It may not change the degree title on the diploma.

Before pursuing one:

- Check whether your program offers it.
- Confirm required courses or research areas.
- Ask whether it fits your graduation plan and supervisor expectations.

## Program transfer

RPG students may consider changing from MPhil to PhD, PhD to MPhil, or moving between supervisor groups. These processes require official forms and approvals. They can affect funding, timeline, visa, coursework, and graduation requirements.

For any transfer, speak with the department and relevant HKUST office before making assumptions.

## Checking graduation progress

Student Center may provide a Graduation Advisement Report or similar function to help students see completed and remaining requirements. Treat it as a useful tracking tool, but confirm edge cases with your department.

## Practical tip

Make your own RPG requirement tracker in the first month: required courses, PDC, GTA, language, seminars, PQE, thesis research, and department-specific items. Review it with your supervisor or department office before course add/drop ends.

For academic policy, graduation requirements, course codes, deadlines, PQE, English requirements, TA requirements, and program transfer rules, always verify with HKUST official sources and your department.
