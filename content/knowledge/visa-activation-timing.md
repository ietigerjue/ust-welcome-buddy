This note helps HKUST freshmen think about when to come to Hong Kong to activate a student visa. It is not legal advice. Visa, entry, permanent residency, HKID, IANG, and immigration rules must be verified with Hong Kong Immigration Department and HKUST official guidance.

## What "activating a student visa" means

When entering Hong Kong from Mainland China, students usually pass both Mainland and Hong Kong immigration checks. The Mainland side checks Mainland travel documents such as the Exit-entry Permit and relevant endorsement. The Hong Kong side checks whether the Hong Kong entry permission or student visa is valid.

The Mainland D endorsement itself does not need a separate "activation" step in the same sense. The Hong Kong student visa is normally activated when you enter Hong Kong using it. At the Hong Kong immigration counter, staff may process the student visa and issue a landing slip.

The landing slip, often called "小白条", is important. It shows the permitted stay period under the Hong Kong visa. Keep it carefully, because HKUST registration may require students to upload it after arrival.

## Why timing matters

Students often ask whether they should come to Hong Kong early or wait until orientation and classes are close.

Things to consider:

- Visa validity: activating the student visa earlier usually starts the permitted stay period earlier.
- Future planning: some students think about IANG or long-term Hong Kong residence planning, but these cases are very individual.
- Housing: if your rental period starts early, arriving earlier may help you handle move-in, utilities, and setup.
- Orientation: many HKUST, school, department, program, and student society activities happen before classes, but schedules vary by year and program.
- Registration: some HKUST registration steps can be started online, but uploading the landing slip requires actual entry to Hong Kong.
- HKID: students who are required to apply for a Hong Kong Identity Card should follow the current Immigration Department requirement and book as early as practical after arrival.

## If you enter Hong Kong early and leave again

Some students may enter Hong Kong once to activate the student visa, then return home and come back later for school. The source discusses this as a practical possibility, but students should be careful:

- Keep all immigration documents and landing slips.
- Make sure your travel documents remain valid for the next entry.
- Book HKID and registration-related tasks according to official requirements.
- If asked by authorities, explain your situation honestly and show appointment or travel records when relevant.

## Registration and arrival planning

For HKUST registration, students may need to:

- Activate the HKUST student account after receiving the registration invitation.
- Fill in online registration information.
- Upload available identity and travel documents.
- Upload the landing slip after entering Hong Kong with the student visa.
- Follow instructions for collecting the HKUST card.

Program-specific orientation, school-level events, and department briefings may be important. Some may be optional, while some program briefings may be expected or required. Always follow your own department or program emails.

## Practical tip

Do not decide your arrival date based only on one factor. Make a small timeline with: visa readiness, flight/train ticket, housing move-in, HKID appointment, HKUST registration, orientation, and course selection.

For visa, HKID, IANG, permanent residency, legal stay period, official deadlines, and immigration procedures, always verify with Hong Kong Immigration Department and HKUST official sources.
