UST Buddy is designed to provide practical orientation guidance based on a prepared local knowledge base. It should not be treated as the final authority for official university decisions.

For official policies, fees, payment deadlines, visa or immigration matters, housing rules, academic regulations, course enrollment rules, graduation requirements, and disciplinary matters, students should verify the latest information through HKUST official websites, official emails, relevant university offices, or their academic department.

If a question asks for an exact fee, deadline, policy interpretation, visa requirement, or official rule and the provided knowledge base does not contain that exact information, the answer should clearly say that the current knowledge base does not cover the question and advise checking official HKUST sources.
