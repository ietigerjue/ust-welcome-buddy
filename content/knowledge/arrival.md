## 中文

### 直接回答

新生从内地或海外来港，可以根据出发地和行李情况选择飞机、高铁、陆路口岸、跨境巴士或轮渡。第一次来港、行李较多的同学，通常优先选择路线简单、换乘少的方式；如果行李较少，可以考虑机场巴士、港铁或高铁转公共交通。

### 出入境手续

从内地到香港通常会经过两个检查环节：

- 内地口岸区：中国边检主要检查出境证件，例如港澳通行证和签注。已录入指纹的同学，部分口岸可使用自助通道或人脸识别。
- 香港口岸区：香港入境事务处主要检查入境和逗留资格，例如香港学生签证/进入许可和港澳通行证。学生本人和同行亲友可能需要走不同的人工通道。

已经拿到香港身份证的同学，可以按香港入境事务处指引登记 e-Channel / 非触式 e-道，之后过关会更方便。具体登记步骤应以香港入境事务处官方信息为准。

### 常见来港路线

#### 1. 飞机：香港国际机场

香港只有一个主要国际机场。抵达香港国际机场后，可以按指示牌前往的士站、机场快线或巴士站。

从机场到科大或科大周边，常见选择包括：

- 机场快线：可到九龙站、青衣站、香港站等，再转港铁或的士。
- 城巴机场快线：部分 A 字头或 NA 字头机场巴士可前往钻石山、彩虹、坑口、宝琳、将军澳、康城、油塘等方向，再转巴士、小巴或的士去科大。
- 的士：路线最简单，适合第一次来港、行李较多或晚上抵达的同学。

Practical tip：如果是第一次来港并且行李很多，优先考虑的士；如果行李较少，机场巴士通常换乘少、性价比较高。

#### 2. 高铁：香港西九龙站

坐高铁可抵达香港西九龙站。购票方式与内地高铁类似，可使用 12306 或香港西九龙相关购票渠道。

到科大周边的常见方式：

- 从香港西九龙站步行到柯士甸站，乘屯马线到钻石山，再转 91/91M 巴士到科大。
- 到钻石山后也可转观塘线到调景岭，再转将军澳线前往将军澳、康城、坑口、宝琳等区域。
- 行李较多时，可以从西九龙直接打车。

#### 3. 陆路口岸加港铁：罗湖 / 落马洲

深圳的罗湖口岸和福田口岸分别连接香港东铁线的罗湖站和落马洲站。过关后可以直接进入港铁系统。

到科大周边的常见方式：

- 罗湖站或落马洲站乘东铁线到九龙塘，转观塘线到钻石山，再乘 91/91M 巴士到科大。
- 也可以从九龙塘转观塘线到调景岭，再转将军澳线前往将军澳、康城、坑口、宝琳等区域。
- 行李较多时，可在港铁站附近寻找的士站打车。

Practical tip：东铁线有头等车厢。乘坐前需要用八达通在头等核准器拍卡，否则可能被罚款。是否乘坐头等、票价和罚款规则请以港铁官方信息为准。

#### 4. 陆路口岸加跨境巴士

如果口岸附近没有方便的港铁站，可以考虑跨境巴士。常见相关口岸包括落马洲/皇岗、文锦渡、沙头角、深圳湾、港珠澳大桥、香园围/莲塘等。

常见跨境巴士服务包括：

- 环岛中港通
- 中旅巴士
- 港珠澳大桥穿梭巴士

港珠澳大桥口岸离机场较近，部分机场巴士路线也会经过港珠澳大桥站点。具体路线、班次和票价应以运营商官方信息为准。

#### 5. 轮渡

香港也可以通过轮渡入境。常见港口包括中国客运码头和港澳客运码头，可能连接澳门、蛇口、珠海、中山、顺德、南沙等地。

轮渡适合从珠三角部分城市出发的同学，但班次、行李规则和抵港后的转乘安排需要提前确认。

### 行李和交通提示

- 香港公共巴士一般可以带行李箱，上车后应放在指定行李区域。
- 小巴是否允许带大件行李，要看车辆空间和司机安排；大件行李或多个行李箱不建议优先选择小巴。
- 大巴下车前可以按铃；部分小巴可能需要用粤语或普通话提醒司机下车。
- 到科大附近时，可结合 Google Maps、港铁、巴士公司或官方交通 App 查询实时路线。

### 官方提醒

出入境证件、签证/进入许可、e-Channel 登记、交通票价、班次、罚款、学生优惠和路线变化，都可能随时间调整。涉及官方规则或费用时，请以香港入境事务处、香港运输署、港铁、巴士/轮渡运营商和 HKUST 官方信息为准。

## English

### Direct Answer

New HKUST students can arrive in Hong Kong by flight, high-speed rail, land border crossings, cross-border buses, or ferry, depending on where they start from and how much luggage they carry. For first-time arrivals with heavy luggage, a simple route with fewer transfers is usually better. If luggage is light, airport buses, MTR, or high-speed rail plus public transport can be practical.

### Immigration and Border Crossing

Travelling from Mainland China to Hong Kong usually involves two sets of checks:

- Mainland port area: China immigration checks whether you have valid documents to leave Mainland China, such as an Exit-Entry Permit for Travelling to and from Hong Kong and Macao and a valid endorsement.
- Hong Kong port area: Hong Kong immigration checks whether you have valid documents to enter and stay in Hong Kong, such as a student visa/entry permit and travel document. Students and accompanying family members may need to use different counters.

Students who already have a Hong Kong Identity Card may register for e-Channel or contactless e-Channel according to the official Hong Kong Immigration Department instructions.

### Common Arrival Routes

#### 1. By Air: Hong Kong International Airport

Hong Kong has one main international airport. After arrival, follow airport signs to the taxi stand, Airport Express, or bus stops.

Common ways from the airport to HKUST or nearby areas include:

- Airport Express: go to stations such as Kowloon, Tsing Yi, or Hong Kong, then transfer to MTR or taxi.
- Citybus airport routes: some A-prefix or NA-prefix routes go toward Diamond Hill, Choi Hung, Hang Hau, Po Lam, Tseung Kwan O, LOHAS Park, or Yau Tong. Students can then transfer to bus, minibus, or taxi.
- Taxi: usually the simplest option for first-time arrivals, heavy luggage, or late-night arrivals.

Practical tip: If this is your first time arriving in Hong Kong with large luggage, taxi is usually the easiest choice. If you have lighter luggage, airport buses may be more cost-effective with fewer transfers.

#### 2. By High-Speed Rail: Hong Kong West Kowloon Station

Students can take high-speed rail to Hong Kong West Kowloon Station. Tickets can usually be purchased through 12306 or Hong Kong West Kowloon ticketing channels.

Common routes toward HKUST include:

- Walk from West Kowloon Station to Austin Station, take the Tuen Ma Line to Diamond Hill, then take bus 91/91M to HKUST.
- From Diamond Hill, students may also transfer through the Kwun Tong Line and Tseung Kwan O Line toward Tseung Kwan O, LOHAS Park, Hang Hau, or Po Lam.
- With heavy luggage, taking a taxi from West Kowloon may be easier.

#### 3. Land Border plus MTR: Lo Wu / Lok Ma Chau

Lo Wu and Futian border crossings connect directly to Hong Kong East Rail Line stations: Lo Wu and Lok Ma Chau.

Common routes toward HKUST include:

- Take the East Rail Line to Kowloon Tong, transfer to the Kwun Tong Line to Diamond Hill, then take bus 91/91M to HKUST.
- Transfer through Kowloon Tong and Tiu Keng Leng toward Tseung Kwan O, LOHAS Park, Hang Hau, or Po Lam if going to nearby residential areas.
- With heavy luggage, consider taking a taxi from a suitable MTR station.

Practical tip: The East Rail Line has first-class compartments. You must validate your Octopus at a first-class processor before boarding first class. Fare and penalty rules should be checked with official MTR information.

#### 4. Land Border plus Cross-Border Bus

For border checkpoints without convenient MTR access, cross-border buses can be useful. Relevant checkpoints may include Lok Ma Chau/Huanggang, Man Kam To, Sha Tau Kok, Shenzhen Bay, Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge, and Heung Yuen Wai/Liantang.

Common cross-border bus operators include:

- Trans-Island Chinalink
- China Travel Tours Transportation
- Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge Shuttle Bus

The Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge port is close to the airport, and some airport bus routes also stop there. Routes, schedules, and fares should be checked with official operators.

#### 5. By Ferry

Students may also arrive by ferry through terminals such as China Ferry Terminal and Hong Kong-Macau Ferry Terminal. These may connect with places such as Macau, Shekou, Zhuhai, Zhongshan, Shunde, and Nansha.

Ferry can be convenient for students coming from parts of the Greater Bay Area, but schedules, luggage rules, and onward transport should be checked in advance.

### Luggage and Local Transport Tips

- Public buses in Hong Kong usually allow suitcases, which should be placed in the luggage area when available.
- Minibus space is limited. Large suitcases or multiple suitcases may not be suitable.
- On buses, press the bell before your stop. On some minibuses, passengers may need to tell the driver when they want to get off.
- For routes near HKUST, use Google Maps, MTR, bus operator apps, or official transport information to check current routes.

### Official Reminder

Immigration documents, student visa/entry permit rules, e-Channel registration, fares, schedules, penalties, student concessions, and transport routes may change. For official rules or fees, always verify with the Hong Kong Immigration Department, Transport Department, MTR, transport operators, ferry operators, and HKUST official sources.
