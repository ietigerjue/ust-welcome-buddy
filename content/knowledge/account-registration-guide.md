This note explains the practical flow for HKUST account activation and program registration. Exact webpages, deadlines, required documents, and system names may change, so always follow official HKUST emails from Academic Registry, ITSO, your school, and your program office.

## Key terms

Program Registration means becoming officially registered in your HKUST program. It is different from Course Registration, which means choosing courses for a semester.

Common identifiers:

- Application Number: used before registration when communicating about admission or application progress.
- Student ID / Student Number: the student number printed on your HKUST card and used in many campus activities.
- HKUST Account / student account: the account used to log in to HKUST systems.
- Student email: usually based on the student account with the `@connect.ust.hk` domain.

The older name ITSC account is still commonly used by students, but HKUST IT services are now generally under ITSO terminology.

## Registration invitation email

The registration invitation is usually sent by Academic Registry. It may include your student number, account information, and instructions for:

- Activating the HKUST student account.
- Completing the Online Registration Form.
- Uploading identity, travel, and visa-related documents.
- Uploading a photo for the HKUST card.
- Completing additional forms if required for your student type.

If classmates in the same program have received the email but you have not, check spam folders first, then contact your program office or the relevant HKUST office.

## Activate the HKUST account

After receiving the official invitation, activate the student account through the official HKUST account activation page. During activation:

- Keep your student number, application number, and identity information nearby.
- Use a stable network.
- Avoid closing the page midway.
- If the page behaves strangely, try clearing browser cache, changing browser, or restarting the device.
- If the issue continues, contact ITSO support through official channels.

## Online registration and document upload

In the Online Registration Form, fill in information according to official instructions. For Mainland China students, travel document and endorsement uploads may involve the Exit-entry Permit and relevant endorsement page. The landing slip can only be uploaded after entering Hong Kong with the student visa.

A practical way to think about it:

- Initial registration can often be progressed by activating the account, submitting the online form, and uploading currently available documents.
- Full completion may require uploading the landing slip after arrival.
- Follow Academic Registry emails carefully, because they define the official requirement for your intake.

## HKUST card photo

The uploaded photo may be used for your HKUST card. Take this seriously:

- Use a clear front-facing photo.
- Follow size, background, head position, and accessory rules from the official page.
- A photo that passes automatic upload may still be rejected later if it clearly violates requirements.

## Student email

HKUST student email is central for daily study. After registration, check it frequently because schools, departments, library, ITSO, student affairs, and course instructors may all send notices there.

Useful habits:

- Bookmark the official webmail page.
- Set up Outlook or another supported mail client if you prefer.
- Learn how display name and email alias work before changing them.
- Be careful with email alias choices, because alias settings may be limited.

## Two-factor authentication

HKUST systems may require two-factor authentication, such as Duo or Microsoft Authenticator / Entra MFA, depending on the current ITSO setup. Set it up early and keep backup access in mind.

Practical reminders:

- Do not rely on only one phone without knowing how to recover access.
- Read the current ITSO 2FA guide.
- If you change phones, handle 2FA migration before losing access to the old device.

## After registration

After registration, you can access more HKUST systems, such as:

- Student Center, also still often called SIS.
- Course registration pages.
- Canvas or other teaching platforms used by courses.
- Library services.
- Software, VPN, and other ITSO resources.
- Student Center billing and academic information pages.

## Practical tip

Create a short checklist: activate account, confirm email login, set up 2FA, complete online registration, upload available documents, upload card photo, save student number, and check Student Center before course registration.

For official deadlines, registration status, course selection eligibility, student visa document rules, and HKUST card collection, always verify with HKUST Academic Registry, ITSO, and your program office.
